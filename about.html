About

I study people before I design for them.

I'm Manisha Sharma, a product-minded UX, UI and XR designer and UX writer with 3+ years of experience across SaaS, e-commerce and real estate. I design end-to-end web, mobile and AR/VR experiences, and I write the words inside them.

How I think

Find the moment. Fix the cause.

Good design starts before the first Figma frame, with a question most teams skip. What is actually happening here?

When a checkout loses 60% of its users and everyone blames price, I watch the sessions. When new users get lost, I sit beside them and see exactly where they stall. When a brief says make it delightful, I ask what it needs to achieve, then design something I can defend with evidence.

My background in political science taught me to read systems: incentives, constraints, and why well-meant plans fail the same way twice. UX turned out to be the same discipline on a different surface.

The foundation

  • Question the brief before answering it
  • Observe behaviour, not opinions
  • Test assumptions against evidence
  • Decide from primary sources

Applied as UX practice

  • Stakeholder and user interviews
  • Journey mapping and heuristic reviews
  • A/B testing and usability studies
  • Personas built from data
Then the pivot

Flat screens taught me hierarchy, rhythm and restraint. XR asks for all of that, plus depth, distance and the human body. I completed IIT Delhi's Executive Programme in VR & AR (Grade A) because the next decade of interfaces won't sit inside a rectangle.
