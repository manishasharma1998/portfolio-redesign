Work/Go Beyond

Go Beyond: health, food and friends in one app.

A health and wellness app for busy people that brings workouts, regional meal plans and social support together, so staying healthy feels less lonely.

01

The problem

Project overview

Create a comprehensive, easy-to-use mobile app that supports the health and wellness needs of busy professionals and students. It brings together personalised workout routines, varied nutrition plans featuring regional foods, and social support features that build motivation and consistency.

Busy professionals and students want to stay fit, but most apps treat workouts, food and motivation as three separate problems.

What people said
  • Hectic schedules make a consistent workout routine hard to keep.
  • Many people stay more consistent when they work out with others.
  • There's demand for one app that covers fitness and diet together.
  • People want healthy eating without giving up their regional food.
What Go Beyond does
  • Community groups and video calls, so friends can work out together.
  • Personalised workout routines built around each person's goals.
  • Meal plans with a wide range of regional Indian food.
  • Fitness tracking, nutrition and support in one place.

Audience: people aged 20–50 in urban and semi-urban areas, including health enthusiasts, patients and their carers, professional trainers and dieticians.

User

What I asked in interviews

5 one-to-one video calls with people who closely matched the target group, to understand their patterns, preferences and concerns around health and wellbeing.

Can you introduce yourself? Can you tell me about your health routine and habits? What do you focus on most: physical health, mental health or both? Which app do you use for health and wellness? What made you start using an app? Which features do you find most useful in the app? Are diet planning or tracker features important to you? How do you relieve stress or anxiety? Would community forums or social features be useful in a wellness app? Do you need reminders? Do they help you? Do you face any challenges using the app? What improvements would you suggest?

What the survey told me
  • Most people are 18–24 and are active in the evening, so reminders and content should suit that time.
  • Lack of personalisation and no Indian meal plans were the top frustrations, which shaped the regional diet section.
  • Workouts, yoga and diet tracking are the most common activities, so they sit on the home screen.
05

Personas

A

Ajay

Age 21Gender MaleStatus SingleEducation BALocation Delhi

Ajay is a 21-year-old graphic design student who cares about keeping a balanced lifestyle. He wants a healthier relationship with food and to stay fit. In his free time he listens to music and watches series.

Goals and needs
  • A healthier relationship with food and eating habits.
  • Track fitness progress and feel a sense of achievement from daily activity.
  • Tools to track calories and nutrients, with useful insights.
  • Easy meal plans and recipes that suit his dietary preferences.
Pain points
  • Workout plans aren't personalised to him.
  • Little regular contact with people who share his fitness interests.
  • Hard to find nutrition plans and recipes for his specific dietary needs.
  • Finds it hard to stay motivated and stick to exercise routines.
Motivation
  • More motivated when doing activities with other people.
  • Prefers things that fit easily into his life, like at-home workouts and simple meal planning.
  • A supportive network, including fitness instructors and nutritionists, keeps him on track.
N

Noor

Age 23Gender FemaleStatus SingleEducation MBALocation Mumbai

Noor is 23 and heads a department in public relations. She wants to put personal growth first and improve her mental health. In her free time she scrolls reels and sometimes stretches.

Goals and needs
  • Connect with people who share her health and fitness interests.
  • Informative content, like forums and blogs, for guidance.
  • Clear guidance and structure to stay focused.
  • Balanced reminders that keep her on track with her health goals.
Pain points
  • Apps push a subscription before giving her enough value or time to try features.
  • Tracking progress in a journal diary is inconvenient.
  • Feels lethargic because of a disrupted sleep schedule.
  • Wants some customisation, but feels restricted by default settings.
Motivation
  • Finds satisfaction in tracking progress and reaching health goals.
  • Journaling and creative activities help her process emotions and reduce stress.
  • Wants to feel better by putting her wellbeing and personal growth first.
